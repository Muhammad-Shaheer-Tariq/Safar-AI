# New York City, USA: Regulations, Culture, and Etiquette

## 1. Visas & Entry
- Visa Waiver Program (VWP) nationals must apply for an ESTA at least 72 hours before travel. 
- Other nationalities require a B1/B2 Tourist Visa, which necessitates an embassy interview.
- Customs and Border Protection (CBP) officers at JFK/Newark have broad authority to search digital devices.

## 2. Tipping & Hidden Costs (Crucial)
- The price you see on a menu or price tag is NOT the final price. New York City adds an 8.875% sales tax at the register.
- Tipping is MANDATORY in the US service industry. Waitstaff rely on tips for their base wage.
- The standard restaurant tip in NYC is 20% of the pre-tax bill. Tipping less than 18% implies terrible service. 
- You must also tip taxi drivers (15-20%), bellhops ($2-$5 per bag), and bartenders ($1-$2 per drink).

## 3. Public Transport
- The NYC Subway operates 24/7. Use the OMNY system by tapping any contactless credit card or smartphone at the turnstile (currently $2.90 per ride).
- Subway etiquette: Do not block the doors, take off backpacks on crowded trains, and let passengers exit before you board.
- The city operates on a grid system above 14th Street. Avenues run North-South; Streets run East-West.

## 4. Street Safety & Norms
- New Yorkers walk fast. If you need to stop to look at a map or take a photo, step completely out of the flow of pedestrian traffic to the side of the building.
- Jaywalking is ubiquitous but technically illegal; follow the crowd and watch for aggressive yellow cabs and delivery e-bikes.
