# Dubai, UAE: Regulations, Culture, and Etiquette

## 1. Modesty and Dress Code
- Dubai is relatively liberal compared to the rest of the Middle East, but public modesty is legally enforced.
- Swimwear is strictly for beaches and pools. Walking into a hotel lobby, mall, or restaurant in swimwear is illegal and offensive.
- In public malls and traditional souks, shoulders and knees should ideally be covered for both men and women. Cross-dressing is illegal.

## 2. Public Behavior & Laws
- Public Displays of Affection (PDA): Holding hands for married couples is tolerated, but kissing or aggressive affection in public can lead to arrest.
- Swearing & Gestures: Swearing in public or making rude hand gestures (especially in traffic) is a criminal offense and can lead to immediate deportation.
- Photography: It is illegal to take photos of government buildings, military installations, or people (especially women) without their explicit consent.
- Ramadan: During the holy month, eating, drinking (even water), or smoking in public during daylight hours is strictly illegal for EVERYONE, regardless of religion, though hotels provide screened-off dining areas.

## 3. Alcohol & Drugs
- The UAE has a zero-tolerance policy for recreational drugs. Possession leads to severe prison sentences. 
- Alcohol is legal for tourists but only in licensed venues (hotels, bars, and specific clubs). Public drunkenness or drinking on the street is a serious criminal offense.

## 4. Transport & Tipping
- The Dubai Metro requires a Nol Card. Do not eat, drink, or chew gum on the Metro; you will be fined. 
- Tipping is customary. A 10% service charge is usually included in bills, but leaving an extra 10% in cash for the staff is standard.
