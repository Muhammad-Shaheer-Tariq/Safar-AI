# Ottawa, Canada: Regulations, Culture, and Etiquette

## 1. Visas & Entry
- Visa-exempt travelers (except US citizens) require an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) prior to boarding a flight to Canada. 
- US Citizens only need a valid US Passport.
- Other nationalities require a Canadian Visitor Visa.

## 2. Weather & Preparation
- Ottawa has extreme seasonal variations. Winter (December-March) temperatures frequently drop below -20°C (-4°F) with severe wind chill. Heavy winter coats, thermal layers, gloves, and snow boots are mandatory for safety.
- Summer is hot and humid, frequently reaching 30°C (86°F).

## 3. Bilingualism & Culture
- Ottawa borders the province of Quebec (Gatineau). Ottawa is highly bilingual (English and French). 
- Government services and many signs are in both languages. A simple "Bonjour/Hello" is standard practice.
- Canadians are known for extreme politeness. Holding doors open and saying "sorry" (even if someone bumps into you) is culturally expected.

## 4. Tipping & Taxes
- Similar to the USA, displayed prices do not include tax. Ontario adds 13% Harmonized Sales Tax (HST) to almost all goods and services.
- Tipping is customary. The standard in restaurants is 15% to 20% on the pre-tax amount.

## 5. Transport
- Public transit relies on the OC Transpo bus network and the O-Train (LRT). You can pay with a Presto card or tap a contactless credit card.
